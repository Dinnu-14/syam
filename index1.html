About Keerthana Educational Consultancy

At Keerthana Educational Consultancy, we are a trusted education consultancy committed to guiding aspiring medical professionals towards fulfilling their dreams of studying MBBS abroad. With years of expertise in the field, we specialize in offering end-to-end support for students seeking quality and affordable medical education in top international destinations.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between students and globally recognized medical universities by providing transparent guidance, personalized counseling, and reliable support at every step of the journey—from university selection and admission to visa processing, travel arrangements, and post-arrival assistance.
